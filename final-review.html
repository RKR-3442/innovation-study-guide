🧠 9 Key Concepts to Understand

1. S-Curve phases: Emergence (slow, uncertain) → Growth (rapid, standardizing) → Maturity (saturated, incremental). Pattern = slow-fast-slow.
2. Porter Hypothesis innovation offset: Strict environmental regulation → forces innovation → offsets compliance cost → competitive advantage. Holds when regulation is outcome-based, not prescriptive.
3. Stage-Gate kill criteria: Gates must have power to KILL projects (not just evaluate). Key: early killing (cheap) vs late killing (expensive). Gate = decision point with predefined go/kill/hold criteria.
4. Triple Helix overlaps: University (knowledge) + Industry (commercialization) + Government (policy) — innovation strongest when ALL THREE overlap and cooperate. UAE example: Khalifa U + Mubadala + UAE AI Strategy.
5. Modularization enables offshoring: Breaking product into modules with clear interfaces allows geographic separation of activities. Each location specializes in what it does best. Apple iPod = design USA, components Asia, assembly China.
6. Valley of Death: Funding gap between seed (idea proven) and Series A (revenue proven). Most startups die here. Bridge: convertible notes, angel follow-on, government grants.
7. Porter Diamond — 4 factors for clusters: Factor Conditions + Demand Conditions + Related & Supporting Industries + Firm Strategy/Rivalry. ALL 4 must be strong for cluster to thrive.
8. Absorptive Capacity (RAA): You need PRIOR knowledge to recognize and absorb NEW knowledge. "You can't see what you don't already partly know." Firms invest in R&D even when licensing technology — builds capacity to absorb external knowledge.
9. 70-20-10 portfolio logic: Short-term survival (Core) + medium-term growth (Adjacent) + long-term transformation (Transformational). Without 10% transformational, firm will be disrupted. Without 70% core, firm can't fund R&D.

📊 Exam Strategy Formula

🎯 For Every 10+ Mark Question

Introduction (1-2 marks): Define the key concept being asked about. One clear sentence definition.
Framework/Theory Explanation (3-4 marks): Explain the framework with all components (use mnemonics). Show you know the structure.
Application with Examples (3-4 marks): Apply framework to the question scenario. Use global example (Apple/IBM/Kodak) + UAE example where possible.
Critical Analysis (2-3 marks for 12+ mark questions): What are the limitations? When does it not work? Counter-argument? Show depth of thinking.
UAE Application (1-2 marks): Always try to connect to UAE context — UAE National Innovation Strategy, Vision 2021, specific UAE companies or policies.
Conclusion (1 mark): One clear sentence summarizing the main argument and its significance.
