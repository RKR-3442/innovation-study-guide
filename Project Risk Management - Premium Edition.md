## 1️⃣ CORE DEFINITION

<div style="background: #ffffff; padding: 24px; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08); margin: 16px 0;">

### Risk

An uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, has a positive or negative effect on project objectives.

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 16px; margin-top: 16px;">
<div style="padding: 12px; background: #f8f9fa; border-radius: 6px;">
<strong>Positive risk</strong> = Opportunity
</div>
<div style="padding: 12px; background: #f8f9fa; border-radius: 6px;">
<strong>Negative risk</strong> = Threat
</div>
</div>

</div>

---

## 2️⃣ MAIN GOAL OF RISK MANAGEMENT

<div style="background: #ffffff; padding: 24px; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08); margin: 16px 0;">

- Increase opportunities
- Reduce threats
- Improve decision making
- Protect project objectives
- Support value delivery

</div>

## 3️⃣ THE 7 RISK MANAGEMENT PROCESSES <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: 700;">⭐ VERY IMPORTANT</span>

<div style="background: #ffffff; padding: 24px; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08); margin: 16px 0;">

1. **Plan Risk Management**
2. **Identify Risks**
3. **Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis**
4. **Perform Quantitative Risk Analysis**
5. **Plan Risk Responses**
6. **Implement Risk Responses**
7. **Monitor Risks**

</div>

<div style="background: #fff3cd; border: 2px solid #ffc107; border-radius: 8px; padding: 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #856404; font-weight: 700; font-size: 15px; margin: 0 0 12px 0;">⚠️ IMPORTANT</p>
<p style="color: #856404; margin: 0;">Risk management is:</p>
<ul style="color: #856404; margin: 8px 0 0 0; padding-left: 24px;">
<li><strong>continuous</strong></li>
<li>repeated throughout the lifecycle</li>
<li>updated as risks change</li>
</ul>
</div>

## 4️⃣ QUALITATIVE vs QUANTITATIVE <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: 700;">⭐ VERY IMPORTANT</span>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

| **Qualitative** | **Quantitative** |
|:---:|:---:|
| Subjective | Numerical |
| Low/Medium/High | Exact values |
| Fast | Detailed |
| Prioritizes risks | Measures exact impact |
| Uses probability-impact matrix | Uses Monte Carlo / EMV |

</div>

<div style="background: #fff3cd; border: 2px solid #ffc107; border-radius: 8px; padding: 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #856404; font-weight: 700; font-size: 15px; margin: 0 0 12px 0;">⚠️ IMPORTANT</p>
<p style="color: #856404; margin: 8px 0;">Quantitative analysis is not always required because it needs:</p>
<ul style="color: #856404; margin: 8px 0 0 0; padding-left: 24px;">
<li>credible data</li>
<li>specialist tools</li>
<li>additional cost and effort</li>
</ul>
<p style="color: #856404; margin: 12px 0 0 0; font-style: italic;">For simpler projects, qualitative analysis alone may be sufficient.</p>
</div>

## 5️⃣ RISK SCORE

<div style="background: #d1ecf1; border-left: 5px solid #17a2b8; padding: 20px; border-radius: 6px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #0c5460; font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin: 0;">Risk Score = Probability × Impact</p>
</div>

<p style="color: #495057; margin: 16px 0; font-weight: 600;">Higher score = more severe risk</p>

---

## 6️⃣ THREAT RESPONSE STRATEGIES <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: 700;">⭐ VERY IMPORTANT</span>

<div style="background: #1e3a5f; color: #ffffff; padding: 24px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 24px 0;">

### Threat Response Strategies

- **Avoid** — Eliminate the threat
- **Mitigate** — Reduce probability or impact
- **Transfer** — Shift risk to third party
- **Escalate** — Move risk to higher management
- **Accept** — Acknowledge the risk

</div>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

### Acceptance Types

| **Active Acceptance** | **Passive Acceptance** |
|:---|:---|
| Contingency plan/reserve prepared | No proactive action |
| Response prepared in advance | Risk reviewed only |

</div>

---

## 7️⃣ OPPORTUNITY RESPONSE STRATEGIES

<div style="background: #1e3a5f; color: #ffffff; padding: 24px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 24px 0;">

### Opportunity Response Strategies

- **Exploit** — Ensure opportunity happens
- **Enhance** — Increase probability/impact
- **Share** — Share opportunity with partner
- **Escalate** — Move to higher authority
- **Accept** — Benefit if it occurs

</div>

---

## 8️⃣ ESCALATION RULE <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: 700;">⭐ EXAM FAVORITE</span>

<div style="background: #ffffff; padding: 24px; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08); margin: 16px 0;">

### Risk Escalation

Moving a risk to a higher level when:

- authority is insufficient
- strategic objectives are affected
- multiple projects/departments are impacted

</div>

<div style="background: #fff3cd; border: 2px solid #ffc107; border-radius: 8px; padding: 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #856404; font-weight: 700; font-size: 15px; margin: 0 0 12px 0;">⚠️ VERY IMPORTANT</p>
<p style="color: #856404; margin: 0;">A risk is escalated to:</p>
</div>

<div style="background: #e8f4f8; padding: 16px 24px; border-left: 4px solid #e67e22; margin: 16px 0;">
<p style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700; font-size: 16px; margin: 0;">👉 the level whose objective is affected</p>
</div>

---

## 9️⃣ RISK OWNER vs RISK ACTION OWNER <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700;">⭐</span>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

| **Risk Owner** | **Risk Action Owner** |
|:---:|:---:|
| Monitors the risk | Implements response |
| Tracks triggers | Executes actions |
| **Accountable for risk** | **Responsible for action** |

</div>

---

## 🔟 SECONDARY vs RESIDUAL RISK <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700;">⭐</span>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

| **Secondary Risk** | **Residual Risk** |
|:---|:---|
| New risk created by response | Remaining risk after response |
| Caused by mitigation/action | Cannot be eliminated completely |

</div>

<div style="background: #e8f4f8; border-left: 4px solid #3498db; padding: 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700; margin: 0 0 12px 0;">💡 Memory Trigger</p>
<ul style="color: #34495e; margin: 0; padding-left: 24px;">
<li>Residual risk = <strong>REMAINS</strong> after response</li>
<li>Secondary risk = <strong>CREATED BY</strong> response</li>
</ul>
</div>

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ CONTINGENCY vs MANAGEMENT RESERVE <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700;">⭐</span>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

| **Contingency Reserve** | **Management Reserve** |
|:---:|:---:|
| Known risks | Unknown risks |
| Included in baseline | Outside baseline |
| Planned uncertainty | Unforeseen uncertainty |

</div>

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ TRIGGER CONDITIONS <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700;">⭐</span>

<div style="background: #fff3cd; border: 2px solid #ffc107; border-radius: 8px; padding: 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #856404; font-weight: 700; font-size: 15px; margin: 0 0 12px 0;">⚠️ IMPORTANT</p>
<p style="color: #856404; margin: 0;">Trigger conditions activate planned risk responses during the Implement Risk Responses process.</p>
</div>

<div style="background: #f8f9fa; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 16px 0;">

### Trigger Conditions

Indicators showing that a risk response should be activated.

**Example**

If supplier delay exceeds 5 days → activate backup supplier plan.

</div>

</div>

## 1️⃣4️⃣ RISK ATTITUDE vs TOLERANCE vs THRESHOLD <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700;">⭐</span>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

| **Concept** | **Meaning** |
|:---|:---|
| **Risk Attitude** | Behavior toward risk |
| **Risk Tolerance** | Amount of variation acceptable |
| **Risk Threshold** | Exact limit requiring action/escalation |

</div>

<div style="background: #d4edda; border-left: 4px solid #28a745; padding: 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #155724; font-weight: 700; margin: 0 0 12px 0;">📌 Example</p>
<p style="color: #155724; margin: 0;"><strong>Tolerance</strong> = organization accepts moderate schedule delays</p>
<p style="color: #155724; margin: 8px 0 0 0;"><strong>Threshold</strong> = delays above 5 days require escalation</p>
</div>

<div style="background: #f8f9fa; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 24px 0;">

### Risk Attitude Types

**Risk-Averse** — Avoids risk and prefers stability

**Risk-Seeking** — Willing to take higher risks for greater rewards

**Risk-Neutral** — Balances risk and reward objectively

</div>

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ PREDICTIVE vs ADAPTIVE vs HYBRID <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: 700;">⭐ VERY IMPORTANT</span>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

| **Predictive** | **Adaptive** | **Hybrid** |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Upfront planning | Continuous review | Combination |
| Stable requirements | Frequent changes | Mixed environment |
| Waterfall | Agile | Both |

</div>

<div style="background: #fff3cd; border: 2px solid #ffc107; border-radius: 8px; padding: 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
<p style="color: #856404; font-weight: 700; font-size: 15px; margin: 0 0 12px 0;">⚠️ IMPORTANT</p>
<p style="color: #856404; margin: 8px 0 0 0;">Adaptive projects require:</p>
<ul style="color: #856404; margin: 8px 0 0 0; padding-left: 24px;">
<li>continuous risk review</li>
<li>flexibility</li>
<li>frequent reassessment</li>
</ul>
</div>

## 1️⃣8️⃣ SWOT <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700;">⭐</span>

<div style="background: #1e3a5f; color: #ffffff; padding: 24px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 24px 0;">

- **Weaknesses** — Internal disadvantages
- **Opportunities** — Positive risks

## 2️⃣0️⃣ PROJECT vs PROGRAM vs PORTFOLIO vs ORGANIZATIONAL RISK <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: 700;">⭐</span>

<div style="margin: 24px 0;">

| **Level** | **Focus** |
|:---|:---|
| **Project** | Operational objectives and tactical risks |
| **Program** | Integrated benefits across related projects |
| **Portfolio** | Strategic value and organizational priorities |
| **Organizational / Enterprise** | Enterprise-wide governance and resilience |

</div>

<li>Project risks are operational and tactical</li>
<li>Portfolio and organizational risks are strategic</li>

## 2️⃣1️⃣ MOST IMPORTANT EXAM PHRASES <span style="color: #dc3545; font-weight: 700;">🔥</span>

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%); color: #ffffff; padding: 28px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 24px 0;">

- Risk management is **continuous** throughout the lifecycle
- Risks **change over time**
- Adaptive environments require **frequent reassessment**
- Qualitative = **prioritization**
- Quantitative = **numerical analysis**
- Risk owner **monitors**
- Risk action owner **implements**
- Escalate to **the level whose objective is affected**
- Secondary risk is **created by response**
- Residual risk **remains after response**
- Contingency reserve = **known risks**
- Management reserve = **unknown risks**
- Quantitative analysis is **not always required**
- Trigger conditions **activate planned responses**

</div>
